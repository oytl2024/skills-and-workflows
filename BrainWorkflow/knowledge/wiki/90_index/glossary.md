# Glossary

## Alpha

Mathematical expression that produces daily instrument scores used to form long and short positions.

## Data Field

Named platform data series used as expression inputs.

## Dataset

Collection of related data fields with metadata such as coverage, alpha count, and user count.

## Fast Expression

WorldQuant BRAIN expression language for building alphas from data fields, operators, and constants.

## Self Correlation

Correlation check against the user's own submitted alphas.

## Production Correlation

Correlation check against the broader platform production alpha pool.

## Power Pool

Submission path with distinct requirements and a lower stated Sharpe threshold, intended for simpler and diverse alphas.

## Novelty Score

Local pre-simulation proxy for whether a candidate is likely to avoid self and production correlation problems.

## Contracts

- Start performs local preflight and necessary local compilation before creating a mission. Failure leaves no partial mission and shows exact artifacts/reasons.
- Online platform knowledge capture is a separate explicitly requested maintenance task. Start never grants hidden field/operator/template discovery.
- Stop preserves state and in-flight identity; Start on a paused active mission resumes it without duplicate submissions.
- Ordinary scripted recovery remains automatic; code/knowledge blockers use durable event-driven maintenance requests.
- Qualified but unsubmitted candidates live in the machine knowledge vault with alpha ID, expression/settings identity, check evidence, checked time and submission status. Reviewing/restarting does not recheck correlations. Identity drift, explicit invalidation or policy expiry requires refresh; final platform submit may still require fresh validation.
